**Ridge Runner — Alpine Air Rally.** Fly the Kestrel K-9 bush plane through twelve glowing rings over an alpine valley, over the pass, and bring her home to the strip at Alder Field. A complete 3D flight game in the browser: chase and cockpit cameras, a live HUD (speed, altitude, vertical speed, heading compass, throttle, fuel, airframe), a minimap, fuel rings, damage from hard landings, a stall model, synthesized engine and wind audio, and two modes — the timed Alpine Air Rally and Free Flight.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No build step, no network requests: the game is plain static files under `game/` (Three.js is vendored at `game/vendor/three.min.js`).

## Play

- **↑ / W** climb · **↓ / S** descend · **← → / A D** bank and turn
- **SHIFT** throttle up · **CTRL** throttle down · **SPACE** brake
- Mouse steers too · **C** camera (chase / cockpit) · **P** pause · **M** mute

Thread every ring, watch the fuel — teal rings top it up — and land gently on the strip: a hard arrival costs airframe, and the lake forgives nothing. Screenshots are staged from the real game with `?shot=start|play|cockpit|land&seed=7`.
